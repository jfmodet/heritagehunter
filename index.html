HeritageHunter

Genealogy research assistant for French civil-status and family relationship research.

Project status: HeritageHunter is currently under active development.

About HeritageHunter

HeritageHunter is a genealogy research assistant designed to help researchers identify individuals and reconstruct family relationships from civil-status records and other genealogical evidence.

The project focuses primarily on French genealogy and combines identity matching, civil records, documentary evidence and family relationship analysis.

FamilySearch Integration

The planned FamilySearch integration will use the FamilySearch API in read-only mode to search for individuals, inspect known family relationships and assist with descendant research.

FamilySearch information will be treated as research evidence and will be cross-checked with civil-status records and other reliable sources. Family relationships require human verification before being incorporated into the HeritageHunter research graph.

Research Principles

  • Human validation of genealogical relationships
  • Cross-checking with documentary evidence
  • Read-only FamilySearch access
  • No automatic modification of the FamilySearch Family Tree
  • Focus on traceable and verifiable genealogical evidence

Technology

HeritageHunter is an independent genealogy research software project currently being developed in Python with tools for identity resolution, civil-record processing and family graph analysis.
